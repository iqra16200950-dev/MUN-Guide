What is Model United Nations?

🌍

Simulation of UN

MUN (Model United Nations) is a simulation of the real United Nations where students roleplay as diplomats representing different countries.
Instead of just learning about global issues, you act as a country and debate real-world problems like climate change, war, human rights, or even space law, pretty cool right?!

What you actually do in MUN



You’re assigned a country (like Morocco, USA, India, etc.) and a committee (like WHO, UNHRC, or even futuristic ones). Then you:

  • Research your country’s stance on the topic
  • Give speeches (like a diplomat)
  • Debate with other delegates
  • Form alliances (called blocs)
    • Write solutions (called resolutions)
