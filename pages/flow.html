Committee Flow

A complete timeline of how a MUN (specifically UNA-USA rules of procedure) committee session works,so YOU dont have to suffer understanding random stuff like i did <3

1

Roll Call (like 5 mins max)

The chair calls out each delegates portfolio name . Delegates respond with "Present" or "Present and voting"

If you reply with "Present" this means you have three choices towards the voting of the resolution ;

  • 1.You can Votes for the resolution(Yes)
  • 2.You can vote against the resolution (No)
  • 3.You can abstain from voting (No vote at all)


    • If you chose "Present and voting" you only have two definite choices:
  • 1.You can vote for the resoluton(Yes)
  • 2.You can vote against a resolution(No)
  • 3.You CANNOT abstain from voting(Must vote)

    • 2

    GSL-General Speakers List(30-90 seconds per delegate)

    The General speakers list is the main formal opening speech, it allows delegates to address and present their views on the agenda.

    Each delegate gets 30/60/90 seconds(depends on the motion raised) to present their countrys positions and views.This is a very important segment .

    The GSL is non-exhaustable ,meaning it cannot conclude(all delegates do their gsl),if it does ,the committee session may be suspended .This is very uncommon and wont happen most of the times.If it does ,the chair may also continue under chairs discretion .

    It is very important to note that motions are raised for this (motions are formal requests for the beginning or closing of a session/segment)

    Motion:The delegate of [portfolio name] raises a motion to establish the General speakers list with [x] seconds/time per speaker and [x] number of speakers this round"
    3

    Moderated Caucus

    The name is weird right? Anyways moderated caucuses are sessions of intense formal debate on specific sub-topics related to the agenda.For this usually there is also a list and if by the end of conference you havent given a speech u are given the permission to write substantive chits on the topics (same applies for gsl).

    Inmany conferences (especially 3 day coferences) at least 2 topics will be discussed via moderated caucuses.Most of the time the topics are disscussed on official forums before the starting of the conference .

    Motion: "The delegate of [portfolio] raises a motion to move into a Moderated Caucus on the topic [topic] for the time period of [x] minutes , allotting [x] seconds per speaker"
    4

    Unmoderated Caucus

    This the time where you chill and try to get some work done - including making alliances ,interacting with other dels and maybe even starting to write your resolution.This time can also be used for disscussions with other delegates in terms of futher procedures .

    I urge you to use this precious time to form alliances and maybe even get into blocs ;or make one (Blocs are group of delegates with similar view and positions on the agenda,these groups then work together to write a resolution with the ideology of the bloc in mind).

    Motion:"The delegate of [portfolio] raises a motion to move into an unmoderated caucus for the time period of [x] minutes"
    5

    Papers & Draft Resolutions

    After all of this, its time to start workin on your documents. These written document will be marked too, there may be a cap amount of how many additional documents you can write. Deadline and details of additional documents being accepted will be given prior by the executive board.Once the chair staarts the voting procedure on additional documents, delegates introduce their working papers,press releases, MOUs and more (more details in the document section of the website) Then delegates get to vote on weather or not the additional document passes.DUring this time draft resolutions are also presented but voting most likely doesnt take place yet.

    6

    Amendments

    Delegates propose changes to draft resolutions.Friendly(sponsor agrees) vs unfriendly(debated)

    7

    Voting Procedure

    Buckle up ,cause this is gonna get intense .Commitees vote on all amendments,then finl resolutions.Simple majority is required for a resolution to be tabled(pass).If your resolution was tabeled ,CONGRATULATIONSS YOU ARE ONE OF THE BIGGEST REASONS WHY YOUR COMMITTEE DIDNT FAIL! If no resolutions get tabeled ,the committee fails.

    8

    Closing And Awards

    Chairs announce the awards during the closing ceremony :Best Delegate,Outstanding delegate,Honourable Mention,Special mention,Verbal Mention and more !
