Committee Flow

A complete timeline of how a MUN (specifically UNA-USA rules of procedure) committee session works,so YOU dont have to suffer understanding random stuff like i did <3

1

Roll Call (like 5 mins max)

The chair calls out each delegates portfolio name . Delegates respond with "Present" or "Present and voting"

If you reply with "Present" this means you have three choices towards the voting of the resolution ;

  • 1.You can Votes for the resolution(Yes)
  • 2.You can vote against the resolution (No)
  • 3.You can abstain from voting (No vote at all)


    • If you chose "Present and voting" you only have two definite choices:
  • 1.You can vote for the resoluton(Yes)
  • 2.You can vote against a resolution(No)
  • 3.You CANNOT abstain from voting(Must vote)
