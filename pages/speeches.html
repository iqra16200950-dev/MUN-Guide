GSL Speech

  1. Greeting/adressing the committee(1-2seconds)
  2. Hook/Quote(optional)(5 seconds)
  3. Country Position(10 seconds)
  4. Details on agends/actions taken(20 seconds)
  5. Call to action(5-10 seconds)

Sample GSL Speech

Honourable Chair, distinguished delegates

Human trafficking is modern slavery, and slavery was abolished 200 years ago.

The delegaton of The United Kingdom recognises human trafficking as one of the greatest human rights violation of our time.Millions continue to suffer forced labour,sexual exploitation , and modern slavery due to poverty,conflict and weak international cooperation.

The UK has taken strong action through the Modern Slavery Act of 2015,strengthened border security ,ad partnerships with international organisation to combat trafficking networks and protect survivors.
