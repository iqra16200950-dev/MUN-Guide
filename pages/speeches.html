GSL Speech

Learn how to write good speeches <3

  1. Greeting/addressing the committee (1-2 seconds)
  2. Hook/Quote (optional) (5 seconds)
  3. Country Position (10 seconds)
  4. Details on agenda/actions taken (20 seconds)
  5. Call to action (5-10 seconds)

Sample GSL Speech

Honourable Chair, distinguished delegates,

Human trafficking is modern slavery, and slavery was abolished 200 years ago.

The delegation of the United Kingdom recognises human trafficking as one of the greatest human rights violations of our time. Millions continue to suffer forced labour, sexual exploitation, and modern slavery due to poverty, conflict and weak international cooperation.

The UK has taken strong action through the Modern Slavery Act of 2015, strengthened border security, and partnered with international organisations to combat trafficking networks and protect survivors.

However, this crisis cannot be solved by one nation alone.

The United Kingdom urges member states to strengthen cross-border intelligence sharing, improve victim rehabilitation programmes, regulate online exploitation, and address root causes such as poverty and lack of education.

Delegates, we human beings are not commodities to be bought, sold or exploited. We must act together to ensure that freedom and dignity are not privileges, but universal rights.

Thank you.

Moderated Caucus Speech

  1. Direct point (10 seconds)
  2. Specific proposals (15-20 seconds)
  3. Call for support (optional)
  4. Do not make proposals that do not support the committee mandates; this will lose you a lot of marks.

Sample Mod Cauc Speech

Honourable Chair,

The delegate of the United Kingdom believes broad economic sanctions can unintentionally increase human trafficking by worsening poverty, displacement, and unemployment, causing vulnerable populations to be easy targets of exploitation.

Therefore the UK proposes mandatory anti-trafficking humanitarian exemptions within sanctions regimes to protect funding for shelters, refugee aid, and survivor support. We also encourage UN human-rights impact assessments to evaluate whether sanctions are increasing trafficking risks. We suggest that the UN conduct yearly research on rates of trafficking in sanctioned or economically unstable regions to better understand the humanitarian consequences and come up with better solutions.

Delegates, policies designed to punish offenders must not end up punishing the victims.

Thank you.

Tips!

Voice

Clear ,confident ,varied tone.Practice volume for room size.

Practice

Time yourself and practice your speeches . Practice the way you deliver a speech . This will help confidence too

Pacing

Speak deliberately.Pace your speech and pcreate the flow towards every important point point .

Writing Speeches

When writing speeches , make sure your facts are 100% correct,and everything you write in a speech must be relevant to the agend. DO NOT USE AI TO WRITE YOUR SPEECHES!!! The executive board will DEFINITLY know and like ,you might get cooked.
