Everything you need to know before your first conference!

What is the United Nations?

The United nations (UN) is an international organization founded in 1945 after World War II.Its purpose is to maintain international peace and security ,develop friendly relations among nations,achieve international cooperation and serve as a major center for harmonizing the actions of nations.It helps in accountability,transparency and overall development of the world and its people.

Key Facts

  • Founded in 1945 , after World War II, as a replacement to league of nations
  • 193 member states
  • Headquaters in New York City
  • 6 official languages: Arabic, Chinese, English, French, Russian, Spanish
  • Main organs: General Assembly(UNGA), Security Council(UNSC), Economic and Social Council(ECOSOC), Trusteeship Council, International Court of Justice(ICJ), Secretariat
  • These main organs have multiple subsidiary organs,functional commissions,departments and offices,specialized agencies and other bodies.Along with this they may be a crucial part of funds and programmes and contribute a lot towards research and training

What is Model United Nations?

Educational Simulation

MUN simulates UN (or even non un committees) where students become "delegates" representing different countries ,people or organizations (commanly refered to as their portfolio).We research , write position paper (sometimes),debate solutions,give peak speeches and vote on draft resolutions -just like real diplomats.You basically argue professionaly and always stand up for your portfolio-even if your cooked in 15 different ways

Competitive Activity

Conference awards like best delegate,Outstanding delegate, and Honourable Mention based on research,speeches,diplomacy and leadership really fuel the competitive spirit.But always be aware being extremely competitive and aggresive will power your downfall.

Delegate

🪑

Chair(s)

These people literally decide your placement(awards) .They lead the committee,moderate debates,keep track of the time,enforce rules and announce/decide awards.To be a chair you shall be experianced and unbiased -open to every type of delegate but you shall also have the ability to establish authority without coming of as rude,mean,or harsh (this drives a lot of first timers away from MUNs,so be nice kids)

  • Controls speakers list(order and who gets to speak for a certain segment)
  • Manages caucuses-which are basically segments (explained in flow) (can give suggestions , or even ammend motions under chairs descrition)
  • Recognize speaker(make sure no one gets left out and equal opportunity is given - this also means having fair decisions towards placement )(ps.to all those unfair chairs who gave awards to their friends or were biased -i hope they kick you out of your own committee)
📋

Rapporteur,Co-chair,Vice Chairperson,Moderator

These all are basically assistants of chair ,usually rapporteur,co-chair,and vice chairperson are the same thing .The take notes ,manage paperwork,help with voting procedure and even give an external opinion if needed.A moderator might also be present and as you can guess they basically moderate behaviour , chits and other stuff the chair might need help with

  • Records and may even mark documents and other papers
  • Manages resolution process
  • Assists with voting procedures and helps maintain overall decorum
