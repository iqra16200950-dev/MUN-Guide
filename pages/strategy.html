Winning Strategy

Some cool tactics to get placement

Award winner Criteria

25%

Research and knowledge

You need to have deep country policy knowledge,topic expertise and accurate facts .You must have the ability to put forth strog arguments and give a clear answer when attacked by questions (especially in press conferences)

25%

Speeches

Speeches should be clear,persuasive and well structured.Your research must be showcased through your speeches .Make sure it has an equal balance of opinion and facts,do not ever use AI.

25%

Documents

Quality position paper, additional document and resolutio authorship will bring your overall score up .

4 pillars of getting placements

Be active & significant

  • Be active in the committee
  • Try to speak as much as you can.The more points you can put across,the better
  • Do not scream during your speeches, this might actually lead you to lose marks.
  • Dont put or stir up insignificant discussions.Make sure your points are relevant to the agenda

Build Your Bloc

  • Communicate with other delegates and make bonds with them. Use your unmod time wisely for this.
  • Always try to write your own resolution.Being a sponsor gets you marked
  • Try leading ,dont be bossy ,but show you leadership skills.

Control Debate

  • Showcase your research
  • Yeild to allies
  • Try answering as many POIs as you can - this shows your abilities in research and preparedness.

Perfect Document

  • Write world breaking papers
  • Make smart amendments
  • Format your documents perfectly

Bloc Building Strategy

Day 1

Research All Countries

Know every delegates likely position before conference starts.Have organized documents and keep researching before the conference starts.

Unmod

Form/Create core groups

Get 5-7 committed signatories and sponsor as soon as possible. Make good relations with people ,so during voting they will support you.

Top 5 Mistake (Avoid these!)

1

Not researching country policy

2

Speaking too soft,using irrelevant information and speaking for too long

3

Being too shy and making no aliances

4

Poor position paper

5

Being innactive,not being known in the committee
