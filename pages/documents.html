Position Paper

What is a Position Paper? A position paper is a document that outlines a country's stance on a particular issue or topic. It serves as a foundation for the country's arguments and proposals during committee sessions. basically a 1-2 page document summarizing your portfolios stance, position and policies before the conference.It is required by most conferences ,and one must uphold the same ideology they had in the position paper during the conference.

Basic Formatting & Structure

Heading

  • Committee Name
  • Topic/Agenda
  • Country Name
  • Delegate Name/school

Body (3 paragraphs)

  • Context ,state position , background (past actions)
  • Current policies
  • Solution,beliefs ,proposals and conclusion

  • Tip:Make sure to perfectly format it according to the instructions given by the executive board. As a chair i have seen so many papers that had good content but were marked down for fomatting errors.

                    Committee:United Nations General Assembly Third Committee (Social,Humanitarian & Cultural )
                    Topic:Addressing Human Trafficking

                    [Paragraph 3: Proposals-International database, capacity building for developing nations, funding for NGOs specialising in anti-traficking...]

Draft Resolution

Main goal:The draft resolution is a solution based document passed by a committee via votes. All those debates,arguments and discussions were literally just done for this document.This is like the end-goal of it all.

This also a document that decides weather the committee failed or passed.It is usually very hard to write a Draft reso in like 30 minutes, i have had to scrap sooo many resolutions because of reasons such as the formatting was wrong, deadline was missed ,and throughout I've realised that only those who are thorough with the structure are the ones whose reso's pass and get tabeled for voting.

Structure

Preambulatory Clauses

The preambulatory clauses start with the committee name, and then clauses begining with preambulatory verbs.

Every preambulatory clause ends with a comma (,), if any other punctutation ,consider your draft reso scraped :(

Operative Clauses (Actions)

Operative clauses are the actionable parts of the resolution, detailing specific steps or measures to be taken.Please be very careful when writing these, if an operative clause is not within the committee mandates (what a committee can or cannot do),, your resolution will be considered wrong .

Every Operative clause ends with a semicolon (;)

  • Formating:
  • 1. Every preambulatory clause ends with a comma (,)
  • 2. Every Operative clause ends with a semicolon (;)
  • 3. Every sub-clause to a resolution should end with a comma (,) till and unless it is the last sub-clause to the main clause, it shall end with a semicolon (;)
  • 4. Every main clause before starting with a sub-clause should have a colon (:)
  • 5. Full stop at the end of the resolution.
